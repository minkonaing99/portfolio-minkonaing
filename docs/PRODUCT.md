# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and employers evaluating Min Ko Naing for software engineering roles. They need to scan work quickly, understand technical depth, and verify that projects were built with care.

## Product Purpose

Present shipped work as evidence of skill, judgment, and sustained investment. Success means a visitor can identify relevant projects, understand their technical substance, and reach credible proof such as demos, repositories, and product galleries.

## Brand Personality

Confident, technically serious, and crafted. The portfolio should feel calm enough to scan and distinctive enough to remember.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, decorative dashboards, flashy gimmicks, fake metrics, and visual effects that compete with project evidence.

## Design Principles

- Show proof before claims.
- Make technical depth easy to scan.
- Give important work clear hierarchy.
- Use interaction to reveal information, not decorate it.
- Keep every project factual and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-navigation requirements. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Preserve readable type, visible focus states, and touch-friendly controls across desktop and mobile.
